N-Mosaic

I was inspired by the game Proverbs to make my own variant of the classic Mosaic puzzle.

In N-Mosaic, clues have an associated color that indicates the number of cells in it's neighbourhood that are that color.

The neighbourhood of a cell is the 3x3 square of cells that surrounds it, including itself.

Left-Click to color a cell, Right-Click to clear a cell, Scroll Wheel to change the currently selected color. Click the pencil button at the bottom of the palette to toggle Pencil Mode — in pencil mode Left-Click adds the selected color as a candidate mark on a cell and Right-Click removes it.
