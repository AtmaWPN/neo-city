Friction

"friction is king, friction is everything"

Friction is the force that resists motion.

It's important to understand friction because modern society (especially modern technology) is all about convenience, which is the reduction of friction.

Hacker Mindset

"you can just do stuff"

This is incredibly important, but also the hardest to explain properly.

It's the mindset where you feel empowered to do things and change things about your environment and yourself.

The archetypal hacker sees the world as malleable. They think outside the box because they no longer see the box.

Perhaps the hacker mindset is just the elimination of cognitive blindspots. That is, opening one's eyes to paths through the world that have always been open.
